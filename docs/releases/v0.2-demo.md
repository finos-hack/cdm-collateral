## 1. Token identity: the DTI format (PR #75)

`TokenizedAsset.dti` is now a `DigitalTokenIdentifier` (Rune `string(pattern: ...)`): nine characters, an
eight-character base number without A, E, I, O, U or Y that does not start with 0, and a check character that is a
digit or an upper-case letter. The check character's value is not verified, because ISO 24165 Annex A is not available;
upper case only is a noted assumption (project owner, 2026-11-21). The deployment's DTIs `QUSD00000` and `QAGY00000`
broke the rule and were replaced with `QSD000000` and `QGNC00000`. Task `task-dti-format-check`, resolved.

## 2. Completion by the authoritative record (PRs #75, #81, #84, #86)

Interim rules REG-1 to REG-5 (project owner, 2026-11-21, pending the SME in `task-sme-registered-asset-completion`;
brief: [`sme-brief-registered-assets.md`](../analysis/sme-brief-registered-assets.md)): for a Registered or Claim
token (tMMF in the prototype), a settlement or an enforcement is complete only after the record keeper confirms its
register update; the token is eligible only under a control agreement with the record keeper; the record keeper's
record prevails and a mismatch with the ledger is a break.

- `cdm-ext`: `TokenizedAsset.controlAgreement`, `EligibleUnderControl` (REG-3), `RegisterBreaks` (REG-5),
  `RecordKeeperConfirmation.transactionHash`; `TransferComplete` cites the interim rules.
- Contracts: `RecordKeeperRegister` (`RegisterConfirmed`, written only by the record keeper's key);
  `CollateralControl.enforce` takes and emits the hash of the CDM `CollateralEnforcement`.
- Deployment: tMMF is Registered, kept by "MMF Transfer Agent" under control agreement `CA-TMMF-2026-01`.
- Oracle service: `TransferAgent` keeps the tMMF register and confirms each transaction that moves tMMF;
  `PUT /oracle/v1/register/paused` holds it for tests.
- Gateway: `/calls/:id/enforcement`, `/register/tokens`, `/register/transfers`, `/register/confirmations`,
  `/register/movements`.
- Collateral service: `cdm-state` reports `recordKeeper` and `settlementComplete`; `/calls/{id}/enforcement`,
  `/register/breaks`; the same completion and eligibility in the repo, GMSLA and triparty flows (`/repos`, `/loans`,
  `/triparty`), and a triparty profile may include tMMF (`registeredCollateral`).
- Consoles: the call row shows whether the record keeper has confirmed the step and the enforcement, and each register
  is shown against the ledger with its breaks.
- Live specs: `flow-registered-collateral`, `flow-registered-collateral-gmsla`, `flow-registered-collateral-triparty`.

Tasks `task-authoritative-record-settlement-enforcement`, `task-authoritative-record-other-flows` and
`task-console-record-keeper-completion`, resolved.

## 3. Ledger anchors for substitutions and movements (PRs #88, #90)

G25's `AnchoredRecordEnum` gains `Substitution` anchors built by the collateral service (`CdmAnchors.substitutionAnchors`;
`flow-substitution` checks that both parties build the same three Complete anchors) and `Repo`, `SecuritiesLoan` and
`Triparty` anchors for movements. A movement of a Registered or Claim token completes on its anchor's status and the
record keeper's confirmation. The triparty profile's deposits and withdrawals carry no CDM document and keep the
transaction's finality (project owner, 2026-11-22). Not yet exercised against chain data: close-out and triparty
purchase movements of tMMF. Task `task-substitution-ledger-anchors`, resolved.

## 4. Document provenance (PRs #76, #78, #80, #82, #83, #85, #87, #89)

Gap G41 (finding N2): nothing linked a token to the documents and identities behind it. ADR `wp-adr-document-provenance`
(project owner, 2026-11-22): W3C PROV-O JSON-LD link records, hashed with keccak-256 over their JCS canonical form and
anchored on the ledger; W3C Verifiable Credentials on separate P-256 keys for the record keepers' statements.

- `cdm-ext`: `AnchoredRecordEnum` gains `AssetDocument`, `Valuation` and `RegistryEntry`.
- Contract: `ProvenanceAnchors` (`ProvenanceAnchored`, one chain of revisions per link).
- Collateral service: `ProvRecords`, `Jcs`, `ProvCredentials`, `ProvenanceCheck` and `ProvenanceLive`;
  `GET /api/v1/provenance`, `GET /api/v1/provenance/{symbol}`, `POST /api/v1/provenance/seed`. The check reports, per
  link, missing, not anchored, anchor not complete, conflicting, altered, unattested or superseded records, and, by
  asset, over-issuance and double tokenization.
- Cases: tUST (Bearer: custody record), tMMF (Registered: the record keeper's register statement and the control
  agreement), and the aircraft fixtures (registry entries: the registration and each registered interest with the
  interest holder's consent, an interim rule pending REG-8). No aircraft token is deployed: CDM has no asset type for
  one (G40).
- Gateway: `/provenance/anchors`, `/provenance/head/:linkId`, `POST /provenance/anchor`. Consoles: a Provenance card.
- Provenance gate: tUST and tMMF are marked `provenanceRequired`; the receiving party refuses to take a marked token
  whose provenance path is not complete, at the CSA proposal acceptance and substitution consent, the repo buyer's
  affirmation and substitution agreement, the securities lender's booking and collateral substitution, the triparty
  receiver's settlement and the agent's substitution, and the CCP's acceptance for clearing.
- Live spec: `flow-provenance`. The credential proofs are not checked against the W3C Data Integrity test suite.

Task `task-document-provenance` is open; its SME questions are PROV-1 and PROV-2 (`task-sme-document-provenance`).
