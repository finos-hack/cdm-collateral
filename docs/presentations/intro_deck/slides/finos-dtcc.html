CONTEXT

FINOS and DTCC are looking at CDM and blockchain

FINOS CDM

The industry data standard for trade and collateral processes. For collateral, CDM 7.7.0 models agreement terms and eligibility, but no function or event moves collateral.

DTCC tokenization service

Live on Canton and on DTCC’s private Besu network (ERC-20). Settlement and collateral, including on-chain netting, are on its 2027-and-beyond roadmap.

Missing piece: an open, standards-based margin-call lifecycle that connects CDM agreement terms to collateral settlement on tokenized rails.

Sources: DTCC Tokenization Service fact sheet (Oct 2026); docs/analysis/business-case.md §1

Because DTC holds most US securities used as collateral, DTCC’s Besu network is where tokenized DTC-eligible collateral will exist. That is the reason for choosing Besu as the first ledger.
