How to read it

  • CDM score: the lowest coverage score among the capability rows behind the gap (0 absent, 1 enum only, 2 data types, 3 functions, 4 events, 5 samples or tests).
  • Needed by: the console scenarios (../../console/src/scenarios/scenarios.ts) or planned grid columns that rely on the gap being closed. A scenario that "needs" a gap currently does that step in the prototype's own rule engine, outside CDM.
  • Priority: P1 needed by a built scenario (ISDA IM and VM rows); P2 needed by a planned grid cell; P3 quality or breadth with no scenario depending on it; Out out of scope for local extension.
  • Closure kind: what a local extension would add: types, functions, events (primitive instruction, business event, qualification), replacement (a corrected function under a new name, since CDM's own cannot be edited in place), mapping, or tests.
