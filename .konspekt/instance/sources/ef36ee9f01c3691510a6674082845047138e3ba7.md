## Maintainer

G15 (close-out netting on default: elections only) closed in part with EarlyTerminationAmount and CloseOutNetting in
cdmx.collateral.closeout. Choices made: ISDA 2002 Master Agreement Section 6(e)(i) only, with the Non-defaulting Party
determining the Close-out Amounts; Early Termination Amount = sum of Close-out Amounts + Unpaid Amounts owed to the
Non-defaulting Party - Unpaid Amounts owed to the Defaulting Party, positive when the Defaulting Party pays; all amounts
in the Termination Currency, conversion is the caller's. Collateral held by the Non-defaulting Party enters by
arrangement: under security interest it is applied after the Early Termination Amount (up to what is owed) and any
surplus is returned; under title transfer its Value is added as an Unpaid Amount owed to the Transferor. Collateral the
Non-defaulting Party posted is passed by the caller as an Unpaid Amount under title transfer and left out under
security interest. GMRA, GMSLA, CCP close-out and Section 6(e)(ii) remain open, so G15 is partial.
