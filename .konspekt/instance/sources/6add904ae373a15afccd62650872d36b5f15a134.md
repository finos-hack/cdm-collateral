1. For task-repo-income, CDM's on-demand interest payment only records a transfer, so the Repurchase Price would still include interest already paid. What should an interim interest payment do? (Any answer goes to the SME ledger as an assumption.)
   - Pay accrued, reduce price (Recommended): The Seller pays the Buyer the Price Differential accrued to the business date, less any earlier interim payments. The stored Repurchase Price falls by the amount paid. cdm-ext computes it from the payments recorded in the CDM trade state.
   - Amount entered, reduce price: The Seller enters any amount up to the accrued Price Differential, and the Repurchase Price falls by that amount. Allows partial payments and needs one more input and check.
   - Pay accrued, keep price: The payment is recorded and the Repurchase Price does not change, so the Buyer would be paid that interest twice unless the parties settle it elsewhere.
