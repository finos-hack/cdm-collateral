## Maintainer

G14 (custody and segregation are legal terms only; no enforcement step against segregated IM) closed with
Create_CollateralEnforcement in cdmx.collateral.enforcement. Choices made: enforcement is its own event
(CollateralEnforcement, qualifier CollateralEnforced) over CDM's CollateralPortfolio, outside the margin call lifecycle;
the positions taken leave the portfolio, their value after haircuts leaves the balance, and each moves pledgor to secured
party as a TransferState. Preconditions: the Defaulting Party is the portfolio's pledgor, the secured party enforces,
the custodian or control contract has given exclusive control (in the prototype, the control contract reading the
default from the oracle feed), and an Enforcement Event has occurred. CDM's SecurityAgreementElections -> enforcementEvent
is read as: absent or earlyTerminationDate elected means an Early Termination Date designated and reached;
failureToPay elected adds a notified Failure to Pay or Deliver. This reading of CDM's two booleans is an interpretation,
not taken from the Security Agreement text. The realised value (sale proceeds or appropriated value) is the caller's and
feeds CloseOutNetting as collateralHeld. Sale, appropriation and the custodian's own steps are not modelled.
