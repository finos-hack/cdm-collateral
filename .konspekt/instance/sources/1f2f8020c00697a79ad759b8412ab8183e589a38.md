# CDM payload carrier for peer exchange and the MarginCallState fold (task-e2e-wiring)

## Human (decisions, in response to options)

- Next deep step after the per-message CDM binding: **peer exchange + the MarginCallState fold** — the two parties'
  services exchange CDM documents and each maintains the full validated `MarginCallState` via
  `Create_MarginCallState`, rather than validating each step in isolation.
- Carrier transport, after discussion of (A) an off-chain content-addressed store keyed by the on-chain hash vs
  (B) Besu-native privacy (Tessera / privacy groups):

  > we want to stay as close to real implementation as possible, which I htink means B. Do you agree?

  then, on learning Tessera is deprecated and removed from Besu:

  > ok, let's flip to A

- Sequence and scope: **ADR first**, **IM flow first**, **standalone store service**.

## Maintainer (rationale)

- Besu Tessera-based privacy is deprecated (Besu 24.12.0) and removed from mainline (reported ~25.6.0); its successor
  is LF Decentralized Trust's Paladin (Pente privacy domains). A ledger-native carrier — Tessera, or Paladin/Pente —
  couples CDM transport to one ledger's privacy model and conflicts with `concept-asr-ledger-neutrality`.
- Pattern A (CDM payload in an off-chain content-addressed store keyed by the on-chain keccak-256 commitment; the chain
  supplies ordering, notification, non-repudiation and replay) is a production-legitimate, ledger-neutral pattern, and
  reuses the keccak-256 hash (commit `aa342a9`) as both the retrieval key and the integrity check.
- Contract gap found: `CallIssued` / `CallAnswered` / `CollateralProposed` already carry `cdmHash`;
  `ProposalDecided` / `CallSettled` do not. Per-step anchoring requires adding `cdmHash` to `decideProposal` and the
  settle path.
