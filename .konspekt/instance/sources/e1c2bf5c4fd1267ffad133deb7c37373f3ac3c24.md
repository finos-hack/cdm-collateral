## Maintainer

1. Repo roll, cash on the roll date: I ran CDM's roll on a USD 1,000,000 repo with a Repurchase Price of 1,000,875. The securities stay with the Buyer either way. What happens to the 875 Price Differential, and what is the new Purchase Price?
   - Seller pays interest (Recommended): The Seller pays the accrued 875 in cash and the new Purchase Price stays 1,000,000. This matches CDM's sample (same notional) and the task's 'cash settled for the difference'.
   - Capitalise, no cash: The new Purchase Price becomes 1,000,875 and no cash moves. CDM qualifies this as a roll.
   - Reset to market: The new Purchase Price is the securities' market value divided by the Margin Ratio, and the difference from the Repurchase Price settles in cash, as in a reprice.
2. Repo roll, timing: CDM's Qualify_Roll returned true for a roll on the Repurchase Date and false for a roll during the term. When may a repo be rolled?
   - Repurchase Date only (Recommended): CDM's roll and Qualify_Roll are used as they are, and cdm-ext needs nothing for this.
   - Any date in the term: An early roll needs a repo-specific qualification function in cdm-ext, as reprice, adjustment and substitution did, because Qualify_Roll is false for it.
3. Repo roll, terms of the new trade: CDM qualified a roll with the new date alone and also one with a changed repo rate. What can the proposing party set?
   - New date and rate (Recommended): The proposer enters the new Repurchase Date and a repo rate, which defaults to the current one. The other party checks both.
   - New date only: The rate stays the same, as in CDM's sample. A rate change would need a separate task.
4. Repo roll, agreement: who may propose a roll, and can the other party decline?
   - Either; other agrees/refuses (Recommended): Either party proposes. The other party's matching instruction settles it, or that party refuses and the repo stays due for repurchase on its date.
   - Seller proposes; Buyer answers: The Seller requests the roll and the Buyer agrees or refuses, as for a substitution.
