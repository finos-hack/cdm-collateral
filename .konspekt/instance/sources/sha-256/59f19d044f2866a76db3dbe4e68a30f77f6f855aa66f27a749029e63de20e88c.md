## Maintainer

Eight design questions for row 14 (marking). I recommend "a" on all eight.

1. **What is marked.** a: Aggregated per pair (GMSLA paragraph 5.4). For each party as lender: the Market Value of its
   loaned securities at the feed price × each loan's margin, less the collateral it holds at the feed price (the loans'
   collateral plus net margin from earlier GMSLA calls). The two directions net to one figure. b: Each loan on its own.
2. **Who calls.** a: Whichever party the net figure favours: a lender short of collateral calls for more, a borrower
   that has delivered too much calls for the excess back. b: Lenders only.
3. **Threshold.** a: Call only when the net figure passes the calling party's GMSLA minimum transfer amount (row 12:
   USD 250,000). b: No threshold.
4. **Settlement.** a: Through the existing margin-call path, as a title-transfer VM call whose CDM issuance names the
   GMSLA, as GMRA calls do. The delivering party chooses tUSD or tUST. Margin from settled GMSLA calls counts in the next
   mark. b: Top up each loan's collateral on `SecuritiesLoan`.
5. **The record of a mark.** a: A `cdm-ext` `GmslaMarkStatement` listing each loan's Market Value, requirement and
   collateral value, and the net figure; published, with its hash in the call's CDM issuance. CDM's valuation event is
   not applied to the loans, because that would change each loan's trade hash on-chain. b: Apply CDM's valuation event
   to each loan as well and update the on-chain trade hashes.
6. **Collateral value.** a: At the feed price, no haircut; the loan's margin carries the buffer. b: Apply the
   eligibility haircuts as well.
7. **Returns and call margin.** a: A return redelivers only the loan's own collateral (row 13); margin from calls stays
   at pair level until the next mark nets it off. b: A return also releases a share of the call margin.
8. **Live spec `flow-gmsla-marking`.** a: A lends 5,000,000 tMMF to B against tUSD; the operator raises the tMMF price
   to 1.10; A calls B for about USD 510,000, which B delivers through the call; the price is restored; B calls the excess
   back; the loan is returned; every balance ends where it started. b: A single call without the reversal.
