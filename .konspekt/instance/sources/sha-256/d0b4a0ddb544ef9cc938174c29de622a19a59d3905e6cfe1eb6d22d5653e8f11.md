## Maintainer

Implementing the accepted rule (outstanding IM = agreed amounts of settled IM calls less settled returns) as written,
a top-up IM call settles as an IM call and so raises the requirement by its own amount while its collateral raises the
value by about the same amount: the shortfall would never close. The service therefore tags a call issued from the
revaluation panel with purpose "top-up" in the issuing party's workflow store and leaves tagged calls out of the
requirement. The tag is off-chain and local to the secured party, so the pledgor's service computes a different
requirement. "Excluding enforced calls" is implemented as counting only calls settled after the pair's last Enforced
event, because enforcement seizes the whole escrow without changing call states.
