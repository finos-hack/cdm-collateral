## Row 27 design options (clearing a bilateral repo by novation to BANK-C)

"a" is my proposal for each.

1. **When it clears:** (a) after the repo is booked and affirmed, before the purchase settles, so both the purchase and the repurchase settle through BANK-C; (b) after the purchase settles, so only the repurchase goes through BANK-C.
2. **Submission:** (a) the seller submits, the buyer affirms, then BANK-C accepts; (b) either party submits alone.
3. **BANK-C's checks:** (a) the rulebook is in effect, both parties are paid members, the collateral is eligible and the repo terms are valid; a refusal carries a reason and the repo stays bilateral; (b) BANK-C accepts every submission.
4. **CDM record:** (a) a cdm-ext clearing document holding CDM's split event, giving the two new repos (A with C, and C with B); (b) the CDM event alone.
5. **On-chain:** (a) `RepoSettlement` closes the bilateral repo as `Novated`, `CcpClearing` holds the two cleared repos, and each member's cleared-repo count goes up, which blocks leaving; (b) the repo stays in `RepoSettlement` with a cleared flag.
6. **Settlement:** (a) the purchase settles in one transaction through `CcpClearing` (securities from seller to buyer, cash from buyer to seller, BANK-C nets to zero), and the repurchase works the same way on the Repurchase Date; (b) each of the two repos settles separately.
7. **Verification:** (a) A and B each rebuild the clearing document from the bilateral repo and the rulebook, and check its hash before affirming and settling.
8. **Live spec:** (a) `flow-ccp-clearing`: A and B book a repo; the seller submits, the buyer affirms and BANK-C accepts; the purchase settles through BANK-C; leaving is refused while the repo is cleared; the repurchase settles through BANK-C, and leaving works again afterwards.
