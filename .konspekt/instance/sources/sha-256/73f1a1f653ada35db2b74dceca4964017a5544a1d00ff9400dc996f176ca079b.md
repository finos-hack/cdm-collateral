## Maintainer

From the code, these parts follow without a choice:
- `Create_RepricePrimitiveInstruction` splits the trade into a termination and a replacement with a new cash amount and price, so the trade gets a new hash. The securities do not move.
- New Purchase Price = market value ÷ the Margin Ratio, using the D2 function `RepoPurchasePriceForMarketValue`.
- `RepoSettlement` stores fixed prices and a fixed trade hash. A reprice therefore needs a contract change and a new deployment, with the previous one kept as `besu.pre-repo-reprice.json`.

1. For task-repo-reprice, RepoSettlement stores a fixed Purchase Price, Repurchase Price and trade hash. How should a reprice change them on-chain?
   - Add a reprice phase (Recommended): Both parties instruct with the same CDM hash. The second instruction moves the cash difference and updates the stored prices and trade hash in one transaction. Needs a new deployment.
   - Separate cash contract: A new contract moves the cash difference and RepoSettlement only gets a price update, which splits one economic event across two transactions. Also needs a new deployment.
2. For task-repo-reprice, GMRA paragraph 4(i)(i) reprices the repos to eliminate the Net Exposure. Which repos does one reprice cover?
   - Every open repo (Recommended): One CDM reprice event and one on-chain instruction per purchased repo of the pair, each settling its own cash difference. The Net Exposure is eliminated once all have settled, though the group is not one atomic transaction.
   - One chosen repo: The party picks one repo per reprice. Simpler, but one reprice may leave part of the Net Exposure in place.
   - All repos, netted cash: All repos reprice in one transaction with one net cash payment. Atomic, but needs a batch contract function and a netting document.
3. For task-repo-reprice, the task says reprice is a way to settle a margin call. MarginCall can only be marked settled by CollateralControl, which is at 22.2 KB of the 24.5 KB limit. How is reprice chosen?
   - Instead of a call (Recommended): The party with the Net Exposure chooses Reprice or Call margin in the Repo margin card, and the other party agrees to the reprice. No MarginCall record is created and CollateralControl does not change.
   - On an agreed call: A GMRA call is issued and agreed, then settled by reprice. This keeps the call record but requires a new settlement path in CollateralControl, which is close to its size limit.
4. For task-repo-reprice, how is the Price Differential accrued before the reprice date treated? (Either answer goes to the SME ledger as an assumption.)
   - Settled at the reprice (Recommended): Cash difference = old Purchase Price + accrued Price Differential - new Purchase Price. The replacement trade accrues from the reprice date to the original Repurchase Date on the new Purchase Price.
   - Carried to repurchase: Cash difference = old Purchase Price - new Purchase Price. The accrued amount is added to the final Repurchase Price, so the replacement trade needs an extra stated amount.
