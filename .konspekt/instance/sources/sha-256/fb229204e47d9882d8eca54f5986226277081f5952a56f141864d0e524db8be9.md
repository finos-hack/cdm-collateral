## Open items

These are for `task-triparty-trade-start`. Option (a) is the one I recommend in each case.

1. **Order of steps:** (a) the giver deposits collateral with the agent; the giver and the receiver agree the repo's cash terms (amount, rate, dates) as a `cdm-ext` request, the giver proposing and the receiver affirming; the agent allocates from the giver's deposit and builds the CDM repo with the allocation as its collateral; the purchase settles the cash against the allocated collateral. (b) The agent allocates and settles in one step once the request is affirmed.
2. **Required collateral value:** (a) the value after the profile's haircuts covers the Purchase Price; the haircuts take the place of the GMRA margin ratio. (b) The value after haircuts covers the Purchase Price x the GMRA margin ratio (1.02).
3. **Allocation order:** (a) the giver ranks its assets when it deposits, as in the CSA allocation. (b) The agent takes the lowest haircut first.
4. **Concentration limit in the allocation:** (a) a new `cdm-ext` per-ISIN check (the D30 fix); the allocation caps each limited asset at its limit and fills the rest from the next asset, and when the deposit cannot cover the amount within the limits nothing is allocated. (b) Allocate as today, then refuse if the limit is breached.
5. **Where the collateral sits:** (a) `TripartySettlement` holds the tokens, with a free account per giver and an allocated account per repo, which the receiver holds by title under the GMRA; the giver can withdraw only free collateral. (b) Allocated tokens move to the receiver's wallet.
6. **The purchase:** (a) in one transaction on `TripartySettlement`, the receiver's tUSD goes to the giver and the allocated collateral moves from the giver's free account to the repo's account, triggered by the agent's allocation hash affirmed by the receiver; the repo is not booked on `RepoSettlement`. (b) Book the repo on `RepoSettlement` as well, with the agent's account as the security leg.
7. **CDM trade shape:** (a) one collateral position per allocated asset, each with its own quantity; `CdmRepo` gains a reader for this form; the bilateral repos are unchanged. (b) Keep the single-security form and record the allocation only in a separate `cdm-ext` document.
8. **Console and spec:** (a) the giver deposits and requests in its console, the receiver affirms in its console, and the agent allocates in `#/live/T` and shows the allocation; live spec `flow-triparty-trade-start` deposits, allocates under the tAGY limit and settles, leaving one open triparty repo and withdrawing the free deposit. (b) The agent allocates automatically on the receiver's affirmation.
9. **Leftover on the chain:** each run leaves one open triparty repo and its allocated collateral until trade end exists. (a) Acceptable; trade end will close them. (b) Add a temporary agent-only unwind for the spec.
