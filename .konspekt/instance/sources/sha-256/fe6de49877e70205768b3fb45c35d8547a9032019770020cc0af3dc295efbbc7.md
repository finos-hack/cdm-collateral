## Open items

1. **How to hold the terms.** a: CDM's `LegalAgreement` with its GMSLA elections as they are, plus a small `cdm-ext`
   record next to it for the elections CDM lacks (item 3). A `cdm-ext` check covers D28. b: a `cdm-ext` wrapper shaped
   like `GmraTerms`, for consistency with repo.
2. **Baseline elections:** GMSLA 2010, English law, Base Currency USD, standard Market Value; aggregation set to
   `Aggregated`, as you chose for marking; netting of deliveries and the failure-to-redeliver indemnity both apply;
   notification time 10:00 London; automatic early termination not applicable for either party. a: these. b: changes
   you name.
3. **Elections beyond CDM's type.** a: the agreement adds a minimum transfer amount for each party (USD 250,000, as for
   the GMRA) and a default margin for loans that state none (102%). Rebate and fee rates and recall terms stay on each
   loan, as CDM has them. b: everything sits on each loan, and the agreement adds nothing.
4. **Amendment.** a: a `cdm-ext` `GmslaTermsAmendment` recording the agreement before and after, which elections
   changed, and the event and effective dates, with its own terms-change check. On-chain it uses the existing
   `CollateralControl` chain under kind `GMSLA`: no new deployment. b: extend CDM's `AgreementTermsChangeInstruction` in
   `cdm-ext` with a GMSLA field.
5. **Prototype and live spec.** a: a "GMSLA terms" panel in both consoles, the same as the GMRA one. `flow-gmsla-terms`:
   both parties hold the same terms; A proposes a new notification time; B agrees; both services check the result
   against the on-chain hash; then the spec restores the baseline. b: services and API only, with no console panel.
