## Open items
1. **How the terms are represented.**
   - (a) Recommended: a `cdm-ext` type `GmraTerms`. It holds a CDM `LegalAgreement` (type GMRA, with `icmaGmra` present but empty, the two parties, and the agreement date) plus a `GmraElections` record. Everything stays in valid CDM JSON.
   - (b) A `cdm-ext` subtype of `GlobalMasterRepoAgreement` placed inside the `LegalAgreement`. Rosetta allows this, but CDM's JSON readers would load the field as the empty parent type and drop the elections.
2. **The elections and their baseline values.** The pair starts on these terms:
   - Base Currency: USD.
   - Margin Ratio basis: per trade, from the trade's `marginPercentage`. The agreement holds a default that new trades use.
   - Minimum transfer amount: USD 250,000 for each party.
   - Margin transfer: cash or securities by title transfer, due the business day after the call.
   - Net Margin interest: cash margin accrues at the overnight rate the oracle publishes.
   - Income (paragraph 5): income on purchased securities is paid to the seller on the income payment date.
   - Default valuation (paragraph 10): the non-defaulting party sets Default Market Values at a stated valuation time; automatic early termination is off.
   - (a) Recommended: these values.
   - (b) You change some of them.
3. **Amendment (G19).**
   - (a) Recommended: a `cdm-ext` `GmraTermsAmendment` holding the terms before and after, the changed elections, and the dates, qualified in `cdm-ext` as a terms change. On-chain it uses `CollateralControl`'s existing propose and consent with a second hash chain per pair, keyed by agreement (the CSA keeps its chain). This needs a contract change and a new deployment.
   - (b) A small new contract that holds only the GMRA terms hash and its amendments, with no change to `CollateralControl`.
4. **What goes on-chain.**
   - (a) Recommended: only the hash of the terms in force (baseline, then each agreed amendment). The elections stay in the CDM documents the services exchange, as the CSA elections do now. Each service reports the terms as verified when its folded terms match the on-chain hash.
   - (b) Put some elections on-chain too, such as the MTA, so the contract can check margin calls against it.
5. **Prototype and live spec.**
   - (a) Recommended: a "GMRA terms" panel in both consoles, showing the elections, the terms hash and whether they are verified, with a form to propose a change to one election. The live spec `flow-repo-gmra-terms` checks that both parties hold the same terms and hash. It then has A propose a new MTA, B agree, checks both folds, and restores the baseline, as `flow-agreement-amendment` does.
   - (b) No console work in this task: only the service API and the live spec.
