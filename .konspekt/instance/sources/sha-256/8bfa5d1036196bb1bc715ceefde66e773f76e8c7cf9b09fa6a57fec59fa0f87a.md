## Maintainer

Open items (row 17 design; I recommend "a" for each)

1. **Billing period.** a) The rebate is billed from the last billed date (or the delivery date) to the business date.
   b) Fixed calendar months only.
2. **Amount.** a) The rebate accrues day by day on the cash actually held. It is computed with CDM's `FixedAmount` for
   each sub-period between changes in the cash held, such as partial returns, then summed and rounded to the cent.
   b) The cash held at the period end x rate x days/360.
3. **Who bills.** a) The borrower, which receives the rebate, issues the invoice. The lender's service rebuilds it and
   agrees, and the agreement pays it. b) The lender issues a statement and pays.
4. **Document.** Both options produce a `cdm-ext` `GmslaRebateInvoice`, which wraps CDM's `SecurityLendingInvoice` with
   the record amount and `billingSummary` filled in (working around D27) plus CDM's `InterestReturn` cash-transfer event.
   a) Anchor it separately; the loan's trade hash does not change. b) The payment event becomes the trade hash.
5. **On-chain.** a) New `SecuritiesLoan` functions: `billRebate(loanId, periodEnd, amount, invoiceHash)` (borrower, one
   pending per loan), plus `payRebate` and `refuseRebate` (lender). Paying moves tUSD from lender to borrower and
   records the billed-through date. b) A plain token transfer with an anchored hash, with no contract change.
6. **Negative rates.** a) Rebate rates >= 0 only. b) Also allow negative rates, where the borrower pays.
7. **Returned loans.** a) A final rebate can be billed after a full return, up to the return date. b) Only open loans
   are billed.
8. **Console and spec.** a) A "Bill rebate" button for the borrower, and Pay or Refuse for the lender, in the Loans
   card; a `flow-gmsla-rebate` spec: a cash loan with a partial return mid-period, the business date moved, an invoice
   covering two sub-periods paid, and a second invoice starting at the billed-through date.
9. **Deployment.** a) A contract change and a new deployment, keeping the old one as `besu.pre-gmsla-rebate.json`.
   b) Avoid the contract change; this only works with 5b.
10. **Row 18 (lending fee).** a) Build the billing for both rate types now, with the direction set by the rate type.
    Row 18 then adds the fee-specific rules (fee on the loaned securities' market value, borrower pays) on top.
    b) Build the rebate only and design the fee separately.
