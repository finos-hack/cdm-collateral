## Maintainer

While closing G7: CDM 7.7.0 PostedCreditSupportItem -> haircutPercentage is described as "Valuation Percentage means,
for any item of Eligible Collateral (IM), the percentage specified in accordance with Paragraph 13", but
PostedCreditSupportItemAmount computes value x (1 - (haircutPercentage + fxHaircutPercentage + additional)), treating
it as a haircut. Under the ISDA IM CSA a Valuation Percentage is the share of value that counts (e.g. 98%); passed as
such, the formula would give 2% of the value. The corrected function keeps CDM's subtractive formula; callers must pass
the haircut (1 - Valuation Percentage).
