## Maintainer

G10 (exposure netting not modelled) closed with NettingSet and NettedExposure in cdmx.collateral.exposure, and G9 (no
2016 VM CSA amounts) with CreditSupportAmountVM, DeliveryAmountVM and ReturnAmountVM in cdmx.collateral.valuation.
Choices made: a netting set holds one mark-to-market value per transaction, stated from one valuation party and given
as Base Currency Equivalents (no FX conversion); the secured party's Exposure is the sum, with the sign flipped when
the secured party is the counterparty; Credit Support Amount (VM) is the Exposure less an optional Threshold (absent
counts as zero), zero if negative; the credit support held (Posted Credit Support (VM) or Credit Support Balance (VM))
is valued with the G7 functions, so cash in the base currency has haircut 0; Delivery Amount (VM) rounds up and Return
Amount (VM) rounds down, both zero below the Minimum Transfer Amount and less any disputed amount, as CDM's IM
functions do. Close-out netting stays with G15.
