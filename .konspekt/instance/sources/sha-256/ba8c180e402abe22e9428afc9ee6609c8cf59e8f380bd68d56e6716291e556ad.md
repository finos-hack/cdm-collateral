## Maintainer

G8 (concentration limits never evaluated) closed with ConcentrationLimitBreaches(specification, collateral,
baseCurrency) in cdmx.collateral.concentration. Choices made in implementing it: the collateral held and the collateral
proposed are evaluated together; the measure is the value after haircuts, and a percentage limit is a share of the
value after haircuts of all that collateral; a limit applies to the collateral that matches the criteria it belongs
to, matched with CDM's CheckCriteria on one EligibilityQuery per position, and narrowed by the limit's own
concentrationLimitCriteria; exclusion criteria (isIncluded false) carry no limits; limit types Asset and Issuer apply
to each asset or issuer, a limit without a type to the matching collateral as a whole; the other limit types
(PrimaryExchange, IndustrySector, UltimateParentInstitution, IssueOutstandingAmount, MarketCapitalisation,
OutstandingBalance, BaseCurrencyEquivalent, average trading volume) are rejected by a condition, since they need data
the schedule does not carry.
