## Maintainer

G16 (substitution works for repo and lending only and is qualified as a renegotiation) closed in part with
Create_CollateralSubstitution in cdmx.collateral.substitution. Choices made: ISDA CSA substitution (Paragraph 4(d)) is
its own lifecycle (Requested, Consented, Refused, Settled) over CDM's CollateralPortfolio, outside the margin call
lifecycle and with no trade. Consent is needed only where the pledgor's SubstitutionPartyElection -> needsConsent is
true; no election means no consent (Paragraph 13 (f)(ii)). The request needs the original items held and their value
after haircuts no more than the substitute's (the proviso to Paragraph 4(d)(ii)). On settlement both legs move on the
event date, although the CSA allows the return up to the Local Business Day after the substitute is received; the
portfolio positions are netted and the balance changes by substitute value less original value.
Qualify_CollateralSubstituted replaces CDM's Qualify_Substitution for CSA collateral (D5). Eligibility and concentration
of the substitute are left to CDM's eligibility check and G8. Triparty agent (T8) and CCP (C18) substitution remain open,
so G16 is partial.
