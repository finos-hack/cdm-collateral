## Maintainer

Eight design questions for row 13 (loan delivery and return) are open. I recommend "a" on all eight.

1. **The loan document.** a: the shape the CDM check found. One `AssetPayout` marked `SecurityLending` holds the loaned
   securities. The collateral is in a collateral portfolio: an `AssetPayout` for tUST, an `InterestRatePayout` holding
   the rebate rate for cash. The lender books the CDM execution event, and the borrower rebuilds and checks it before
   affirming, as for repo. b: another shape you name.
2. **Return events.** a: CDM's `Create_BusinessEvent` over a quantity-change instruction, plus a `cdm-ext`
   `Qualify_GmslaReturn` that names a full or partial return and resolves the D4 collision. b: CDM's qualifications
   only (`PartialTermination` and `Termination`), with D4 left as recorded.
3. **Collateral on a partial return.** a: the lender redelivers collateral in proportion to the quantity returned, in
   the same transaction, and the CDM trade's collateral position is reduced to match. b: collateral goes back only on
   a full return, and marking (row 14) adjusts it in between.
4. **Initial collateral.** a: the Market Value of the loaned securities at the oracle price × the loan's margin (102%
   by default), rounded up, delivered with the securities in one transaction. b: the parties state the amount when
   booking.
5. **On-chain settlement.** a: a new `SecuritiesLoan` contract. The lender books and the borrower affirms. Both
   instruct the same settlement document, and delivery and return swap securities and collateral atomically.
   Collateral passes to the lender by outright title transfer. b: one party triggers each settlement after both have
   approved the token transfers.
6. **Term.** a: open loans, returnable on any business day, with an optional term date. b: term loans only.
7. **Rates at booking.** a: a cash-collateral loan states its rebate rate, and a tUST loan states its fee rate in a
   `cdm-ext` field. Paying them is rows 17 and 18. b: no rates until rows 17 and 18.
8. **Live spec `flow-gmsla-loan-delivery-return`.** a: A lends tMMF to B against tUSD; B returns part, then the rest;
   a second loan against tUST is returned in full. Every balance ends where it started. b: one loan and one full
   return only.
