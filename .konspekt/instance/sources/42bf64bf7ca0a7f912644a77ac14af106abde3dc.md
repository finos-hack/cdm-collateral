## Canonical (wp-adr-cdm-payload-carrier, review: proposed)

the CDM document of each lifecycle step travels **off-chain in a content-addressed store keyed by its on-chain
keccak-256 hash**. The chain carries only the 32-byte `cdmHash` commitment plus a per-step event; it does not carry the
document.
