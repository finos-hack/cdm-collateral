## Maintainer

G5 (positions and balances not updated by any function) closed with ApplyCollateralEvent(portfolio, event) in
cdmx.collateral.portfolio. Choices made in implementing it: one CDM CollateralPortfolio per pledgor and secured party;
only a settlement step changes it; positions netted per asset (held + delivered - released), status SettledAmount, an
asset fully released leaves no position; one CollateralBalance, post-haircut, SettledAmount, payer the pledgor and
receiver the secured party, moved by the accepted proposal's valueAfterHaircuts on delivery and by releasedValue on
return; releasing more of an asset than is held is rejected. The balance is the value agreed at settlement, not a
revaluation at current prices.
