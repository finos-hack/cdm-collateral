## Open items
1. **Trade type at booking.** How does a party book a buy/sell-back?
   - (a) My lean: a "Repo / Buy/sell-back" choice when booking. The service sets `tradeType`, and the contract stores it with the repo.
   - (b) A separate booking flow.
2. **Income.** How is income on the securities handled during the term?
   - (a) My lean: reuse the existing income flow. The Buyer enters the income per unit and date, and the Seller agrees or refuses. On agreement nothing is paid; the contract lowers the sell-back price. New `cdm-ext` function: Sell Back Price = Repurchase Price − Σ income × quantity × (1 + rate × days from income date to repurchase / 360).
   - (b) Apply the income only at the sell-back date.
3. **Which other flows apply to a buy/sell-back?**
   - (a) My lean: all current repo flows (margin, reprice, adjustment, substitution, roll, pair-off), as for a buy/sell-back documented under the GMRA annex. Interim interest is disabled, because the forward price is fixed at trade.
   - (b) Purchase and sell-back only, as for an undocumented buy/sell-back.
4. **Pair-off across types.** Can a repo pair off with a buy/sell-back?
   - (a) My lean: no; only trades of the same type pair off.
   - (b) Yes.
