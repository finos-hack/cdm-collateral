## Maintainer

The reading is done, and four choices need your decision before I write the ADR. These parts I will treat as fixed unless you object:

- **Exposure (`cdm-ext`):** Transaction Exposure = Repurchase Price accrued to the business date × Margin Ratio − market value at the oracle price. Net Exposure sums it across the pair's open repos in both directions, less the Net Margin already held. This formula comes from model memory of the GMRA and has not been checked against agreement text.
- **Margin Ratio:** read per repo according to the `marginRatioBasis` election.
- **Call control:** the caller's service computes the call and rejects it unless the amount exceeds that party's `minimumTransferAmount`. The called party's service recomputes from its own `TradeState`s before agreeing.
- **Reversal:** when exposure reverses, the other party issues a new call, so `CallKind.Return` is not used.
- **D2:** a `cdm-ext` function computes Purchase Price as market value ÷ Margin Ratio, with tests, as `wp-adr-repo-gmra-scope` requires.
