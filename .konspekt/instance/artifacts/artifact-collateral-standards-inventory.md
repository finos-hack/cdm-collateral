# Standards and specifications for collateral management

Inventory of ISO identifier and code standards, collateral messaging standards
(ISO 20022 colr, ISO 15022, FpML collateral processes, SCoRE), legal
documentation, calculation methodologies, regulation, and model/serialisation
standards, each with its use in CDM 7.6.0. Findings S1–S4: no CDM mapping to
either collateral message standard; ISO identifiers cited inconsistently, DTI,
FISN, ELF and IBAN absent; Financial Collateral Directive and SFTR absent; ISDA
Collateral Asset Definitions at the 2003 edition. External descriptions come
from secondary web sources.
