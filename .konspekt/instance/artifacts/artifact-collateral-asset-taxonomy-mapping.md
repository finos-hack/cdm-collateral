# Mapping between the four asset taxonomies for eligible collateral

17 asset categories mapped across CDM eligibility criteria, ISDA Collateral Asset Definitions (2003), EU/UK EMIR and
US CFTC/PR eligible-collateral classes, and ISO 10962 CFI groups, with notes on where the taxonomies diverge. The CFI
column was verified against two open-source ISO 10962 implementations (pycfi, parse-cfi).
