# Taxonomies in collateral data

Inventory of the classification schemes collateral data uses — asset
classification, regulatory eligible-collateral classes, issuer types, credit
ratings, geography and maturity, sector and index, limits, margin-process codes,
lifecycle events and party roles — each with its external source, its CDM 7.6.0
representation (closed enum, open string, FpML coding scheme or absent) and value
counts. Findings TX1–TX5, also added to the findings document as proposed.
