# Collateral flows by agreement family

Flow diagrams for repo (GMRA), securities lending (GMSLA), triparty and CCP
margin, on the same layout as the ISDA CSA model, with a comparison table of
parties and margin mechanisms. Boxes coloured by the CDM 7.6.0 coverage scores
in `artifact-cdm-other-agreements-matrix`. Generated by
`docs/analysis/diagrams/collateral_diagrams.py`, which now also generates the
CSA diagram.

Each diagram carries one netting box coloured by its netting score (added 2026-10-15).
