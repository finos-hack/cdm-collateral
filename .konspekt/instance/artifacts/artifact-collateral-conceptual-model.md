# Collateral management conceptual model — ISDA credit support documents

Visual conceptual model of bilateral collateral management under ISDA credit
support documents: pledgor and secured party lanes, agreements, the netting and
amount calculation, the margin call negotiation, posting, substitution, the
collateral account and balance, and interest, with the balance feeding the next
call. Boxes are coloured by the CDM 7.6.0 coverage scores in
`artifact-cdm-csa-coverage-matrix`. Drawn by the maintainer; no published
model of this space was found online.
